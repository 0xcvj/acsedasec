Why now

Attackers don't keep office hours.

Small and mid-sized businesses use the same laptops, email and booking systems as large companies, and attackers target them the same way. The difference is that most have no one monitoring their systems outside office hours.

July 2025

The region is a target

Attacks hit the Curaçao tax office, the Joint Court of Justice and email accounts of the Aruban parliament.

After hours

Ransomware runs at night

Attackers launch when offices are empty, so the damage spreads for hours before anyone notices.

One click

Email is still the way in

A fake booking, invoice or password reset is all it takes for one staff member to let them in.

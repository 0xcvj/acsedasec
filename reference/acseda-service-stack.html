How the Acseda stack reads, top to bottom

The same layering as an enterprise SOC diagram, sized for a 25-person hotel or law office in Aruba. Clients buy what's at the top. Everything underneath is resold vendor tech, with Acseda as the local layer that owns the relationship.

Acseda One
The XDR Lite / managed SOC. Sold as an alarm system for the hours nobody is in the office. $18–25/device/mo, $299 minimum. Highest liability. Needs Acseda's own professional liability cover before the first client.
Acseda Aware
Monthly simulated phishing plus short training for whoever clicks. $4–6/user/mo. Resold platform. A few hours of setup, then mostly automatic.
Acseda Browse
Password vault, link scanner and safe file transfer, bundled into one install. $9–12/user/mo. No single product does this today. Bundle existing tools first; never build the vault.
Secure Check
The free onboarding assessment that opens every deal. Free, no consultation fee. The "triage, diagnose" step from the sales framework. Competitors also offer free first scans, so this one has to show a real result: device list, risks, and a fix plan.
Acseda Desk
The local human layer: escalations, explaining incidents, the monthly report. This is what an overseas vendor cannot offer. Extra support billed per hour, $90–110/h in Aruba.
Platform layers
Detection is the partner MDR (Huntress, Blackpoint, SentinelOne, vendor still open). Compliance turns its data into GDPR-grade, PCI-DSS and EU-guest reports for hotels.

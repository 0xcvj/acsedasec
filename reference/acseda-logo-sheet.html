The watching owl

An owl sees in the dark, which is the promise of Acseda One: someone watching while the office is closed. The mark is a solid owl head with two open eyes and a yellow beak. Royal blue carries the brand; yellow is only for small highlights.

Usage

  • Clear spaceKeep at least one eye's width of empty space around the logo.
  • Minimum sizeFull lockup 120px wide; icon alone down to 16px.
  • BackgroundsWhite, royal blue or navy. On busy photos, place the logo on a navy or royal blue panel.
  • Don'tRecolour the eyes, stretch the owl, or set the wordmark in another typeface.
  • Wordmark fontSora ExtraBold, tracked +4%. Copied SVGs reference the font; outline the text before sending to a printer.
  • StatusConcept for exploration. Check trademark and the acsedasec.com domain before using it publicly.

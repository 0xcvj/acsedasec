Terms and limits

  • Contract12 months, billed monthly. Users and devices can be added any month.
  • Upgrade pathEssentials clients can move to One at any time; the setup is already half done.
  • Extra Desk time$100 per hour, in 30-minute blocks. Night call-outs outside a critical incident are billed at 1.5×.
  • Not includedFull data recovery, forensic investigations, network or hardware projects, and compliance certification. Quoted separately.
  • InsuranceAcseda reports help with cyber insurance applications, but do not guarantee cover or a lower premium.
  • Data locationVault and filter data hosted in an EU region, in line with the GDPR-grade promise.
